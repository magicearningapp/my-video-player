# My Video Player

A simple, lightweight and ad-free offline video player for Android.

## Features

- 🚫 No advertisements
- 📱 Optimized for Android
- ▶️ Smooth video playback
- 📂 Browse videos from device storage
- 🔍 Search videos
- ❤️ Favorites
- 🕐 Recently played
- ⏯️ Resume playback
- ⏩ Playback speed control
- 🔆 Brightness control
- 🔊 Volume control
- 🔒 Screen lock
- 🌙 Dark mode
- 📺 Fullscreen playback
- 🔌 Works completely offline

## Privacy

My Video Player is designed with privacy in mind.

- Videos remain on your device
- No video uploads
- No account required
- No advertising SDK
- No analytics
- No unnecessary internet access

## Technology

- Kotlin
- Jetpack Compose
- Material 3
- Android Media3 / ExoPlayer
- Android MediaStore

## Target Device

Designed and tested for Android smartphones, including realme C65.
